    Notice F

    Questions drivers ask

    Q.What is a T-permit car?
    A.

    A car registered for commercial use with a yellow number plate. Only a T-permit car can legally carry paying passengers on the apps.

    Q.Can I attach my private car to the apps?
    A.

    A private car has to be converted to commercial registration first, with a permit, fitness certificate and commercial insurance. We tell you if your car qualifies and handle the RTO steps.

    Q.How much down payment do I need?
    A.

    It depends on the car and the lender. Many buyers start at around 10% of the on-road price. Use the worksheet above for an estimate and ask us for this month's offer.

    Q.Do I need a badge to drive a cab?
    A.

    Yes. Maharashtra asks every commercial passenger driver for a badge. It needs proof of Maharashtra domicile and practical knowledge of Marathi. We help you prepare the application.
